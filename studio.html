Neural data, de-identified

Remove the disease fingerprint from EEG. Keep everything the study needs.

SANE neutralizes subject-level spectral and spatial biomarkers, such as an elevated theta/beta ratio, while preserving trial-level brain dynamics, events and auxiliary channels. You get the data back in the same file format.

What happens to your data

  1. 1
    ReadEvery recording is parsed with MNE-Python. Scalp EEG is separated from EOG, ECG, EMG and trigger channels, which pass through untouched.
  2. 2
    NeutralizeEach subject's band power is set to the reference, and the per-band spatial covariance is aligned with a minimal-distortion optimal-transport map.
  3. 3
    ReturnDownload the result as EDF, BDF, BrainVision, EEGLAB or FIF, matching the file you opened, with the same sampling rate, channels and events.
Your recordings never leave this computer: SANE runs inside your browser, and closing the tab clears everything.
Header identifiers such as name, birth date and measurement date can be stripped from the output.
A privacy report measures what changed: biomarker deviation, spatial fingerprint and the Trait Leakage Index.
